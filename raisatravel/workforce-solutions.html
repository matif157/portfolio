How we work with you

One channel, named people.

Corporate hiring does not fail on candidate quality. It fails on communication — three people each sending a different version of the same status, and nobody certain what is actually blocking a deployment.

So we keep it narrow. One consultant owns your account, you get a direct line to them, and escalation goes to the Director HR or the CEO rather than into a queue.
