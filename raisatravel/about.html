About the company

Twenty-nine years of placing people, on paper and in person.

Raisa Recruitment Group & Travel Services has promoted overseas employment since 1997. It is a licensed Overseas Employment Promoter, a member of the Faisalabad Chamber of Commerce and Industries, and one of the very few firms in the region operating a dedicated head hunting department.

What the company actually does

Raisatravel supplies skilled and unskilled manpower to multinational companies overseas. The work is not recruitment in the narrow sense — it is the entire chain that ends with a worker standing on the right site, on the right date, legally documented.

The company takes the view that a placement fails if the medical is not cleared in the destination country, the visa is not lodged with the right consulate, or the candidate arrives without knowing what the role involves. So those steps sit inside the same procedure, under the same supervision.

That approach is also why the firm keeps sector consultants rather than general recruiters. A consultant who has placed welders into a Saudi fabrication shop reads a resume differently from one who has never seen the site.

Continuity with clients is the measure the company holds itself to. It reports a spotless record since 1997 and states that a reference can be provided from any client on request — a claim worth making only if you expect to be held to it.

Our journey

Built one year at a time.

The company's own record, kept as a narrative rather than a brochure.

01
1997

Raisa Travel Service is founded

Established in Faisalabad to promote overseas employment. The licence and the chamber membership that followed made the firm one of a small number of properly registered promoters operating at that scale outside Karachi and Lahore.

02
1998

The long-serving core forms

Ali Nawaz joins as Manager Administration and remains the longest-serving member of the team. Continuous senior staffing through the following decades became the reason client relationships survived changes in the market.

03
2004

A new leadership structure takes over placements

Muhammad Zulfiqar Shahzad moves into the chief executive role, bringing three decades of recruitment experience across the private and public sectors. Noman Nisar joins as Director HR the same month. Between them the two still run the company's overseas mandates.

04
2006

Records go digital

Muhammad Qasim joins and builds an integrated office automation programme that moves candidate records to a paperless system — at a point when most agencies in the sector were still filing by hand.

05
Today

A B2B supply partner for the Gulf

Thirty-one staff and ten consultants work across four markets — Saudi Arabia, Dubai, Kuwait and Malaysia — with 31 staff including a dedicated head hunting department, an in-house interview and examination facility, and a licence valid through December 2026.
