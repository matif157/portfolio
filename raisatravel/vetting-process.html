Nine documented steps. Nothing reaches you unchecked.

A deployment is not a single hire. It is a medical cleared in one country, a visa lodged with another, and a worker arriving on the right date. Two filters produce that result: a four-stage screen on your requirement, then a five-stage procedure on every candidate who survives it.

The intake gate

Your requirement is screened first.

Before a single profile reaches you, the requirement itself is put through four checks. Most rejections happen here rather than at interview stage — and they are cheap to catch, because they are usually a mismatch between the job description and the destination market's rules.

We would rather tell you on day one that a role is not workable than take your budget and return a shortlist three weeks later.

01

JD Matching

Your job description is matched line by line against the destination market's requirements and against our live pool. A role needing a licence we cannot verify, or a salary the market will not carry, is flagged here.

02

Education Check

Degrees, diplomas and trade certificates are verified with the issuing institution rather than accepted from a resume. Documents that cannot be confirmed do not go in a shortlist.

03

Skill Evaluation

Claimed competence is put to a practical test in our own facility in Faisalabad — a written test tells you very little about whether someone can weld, wire or tile to the destination's tolerance.

04

Shortlisting

Only candidates who clear all three checks reach you, with the evidence attached. What you receive is a defensible list, not a forwarded stack of unverified resumes.

Stage by stage

From submission to arrival

Stage 01

Submission

Candidate Raisatravel

Applications and current resumes are logged against your requisition and assessed before anything else happens. The first filter is not the CV — it is whether the role, destination and contract type are actually workable.

  • Resume logged against a named requisition
  • Destination market and contract type confirmed
  • Trade certificates and licences verified where relevant
  • Candidate told what the role and package involve — no false promises
Stage 02

Shortlisting

Raisatravel Telephone confirmed

Profiles are filtered against your requirement and then verified by telephone. A resume that cannot be confirmed is removed from the list — this is the stage where most agencies quietly pass along unverified numbers.

  • Shortlist built against your criteria, not a generic pool
  • Every profile confirmed reachable and willing
  • Salary expectations checked against the destination package
  • Unavailable or already-deployed candidates removed
Stage 03

Selection

Interview or trade test In-house facility

Selection runs in our own interview and examination rooms in Faisalabad. Your team can send its own panel, or use ours. For trades, the test is practical rather than a written test.

  • Interview or trade test conducted on record
  • Available on site in Faisalabad — candidates are not asked to travel
  • Results shared with you before any commitment is made
  • Failed candidates can be re-assessed later without losing their file
Stage 04

Medical

Destination country rules Pre-departure

Selected candidates are examined at a facility nominated by the destination country. This is a legal gate, not a formality — a failed medical ends the placement regardless of how good the candidate was.

  • Examination at a facility nominated by the destination country
  • Tests performed to that country's own requirements
  • Medical reports retained with the deployment file
  • Candidates who fail given an honest explanation, not a rejection slip
Stage 05

Processing

Consulate Dispatch

The passport goes to the consulate with the correct paperwork, and dispatch is arranged once the visa clears. You are told the expected arrival date, and you get told when it changes.

  • Passport lodged with the destination consulate
  • Visa tracked to issue rather than to submission
  • Flight and travel arranged against your contract date
  • Handover confirmed to you once the worker has arrived

Common questions

Before you send a requirement

How long does a deployment usually take?

It depends on whether the medical or the visa is the bottleneck in your destination. Ask for an indicative timeline when you send the requirement — we will give you one, with the stages that usually cause delay named.

Can you source a trade we do not already list?

Yes. The sectors on this site are where our consultants have depth, not a closed list. The head hunting department exists precisely for the roles that do not fit a standard pattern.

Do you charge the candidate or the employer?

As a licensed Overseas Employment Promoter we work to the Bureau of Emigration & Overseas Employment framework, which sets what may be charged on each side. The commercial terms are confirmed in writing before any search begins.

What happens to a candidate who fails the medical?

The placement ends, and we tell them why rather than leaving them to guess. Their file is retained, because a candidate who fails on a technicality one month may clear it the next, and re-screening starts from their file rather than from zero.

How do we verify you are legitimate?

Check the OEP licence number 0669/LHR against the Bureau of Emigration & Overseas Employment register, ask for our client references, or call the office on +92 41 2637032 and ask for the CEO by name.

Send us a role. We will tell you if we can fill it.

Include the destination market and contract type. That is usually all we need for a useful first answer.
