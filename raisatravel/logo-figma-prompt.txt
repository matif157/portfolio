RAISATRAVEL - LOGO SPECIFICATION
================================

Brand
-----
Raisatravel - global manpower and recruitment agency.
Supplies skilled workforce to large international corporate groups
across the Gulf & Middle East. Est. 1997.

Positioning: premium, enterprise-grade, trustworthy, precise.


WHAT WAS BUILT
--------------
The mark lives at: public/assets/svg/logo-mark.svg
The wordmark is set in HTML/CSS (not outlined paths) so it stays
crisp at every size and can be edited without touching the SVG.

Files:
  public/assets/svg/logo-mark.svg   -> the symbol / favicon
  src/css/layout.css                -> .brand-wordmark typography

Lockup used in header and footer:
  [mark] RAISA TRAVEL
  where RAISA is white and TRAVEL is cyan (#00e0ff)


THE MARK
--------
Concept: converging routes rising to a single node.

  - One cyan circle at the top = the destination / the placement.
  - Two cyan strokes rising from the lower left and lower right and
    converging on that node = candidate routes merging into one
    reliable outcome.
  - Two shorter white strokes at 32% opacity beneath them = the wider
    pipeline behind the shortlisted few.

Reads as: connection, convergence, ascent, precision.
Deliberately avoids: globes, hands, figures, briefcases, swooshes.

Geometry (64x64 grid, stroke-based, stroke-width 5.5, round caps):

  <circle cx="32" cy="11" r="5.5" fill="#00e0ff"/>
  <path d="M9 43.5 L32 20.5" stroke="#00e0ff" stroke-width="5.5" stroke-linecap="round"/>
  <path d="M55 43.5 L32 20.5" stroke="#00e0ff" stroke-width="5.5" stroke-linecap="round"/>
  <path d="M14 58 L32 40" stroke="#ffffff" stroke-width="5.5" stroke-linecap="round" opacity="0.32"/>
  <path d="M50 58 L32 40" stroke="#ffffff" stroke-width="5.5" stroke-linecap="round" opacity="0.32"/>

Because it is stroke-only with round caps it stays legible down to
16px, which is why it doubles as the favicon without a second asset.


COLOUR
------
  Deep navy (background)  #000814
  White                   #ffffff
  Accent cyan             #00e0ff

Cyan appears on the mark and on the wordmark tail only. No gradients.


WORDMARK
--------
  Typeface:   Inter 700 (Google Fonts, already loaded site-wide)
  Tracking:   0.16em
  Case:       all caps
  Colour:     RAISA = #ffffff, TRAVEL = #00e0ff
  Size:       clamp(0.95rem, 1.4vw, 1.1rem)

Inter was chosen because it is already in the build, has excellent
legibility at small sizes, and its wide apertures hold up next to a
geometric stroke-based mark. If the brand is later refreshed, Space
Grotesk or Sora would pair equally well at the same tracking.


VARIANTS TO PRODUCE IF THE BRAND IS EVER EXTENDED
-------------------------------------------------
  1. Primary       mark + wordmark on light background (navy wordmark)
  2. Reversed      mark + wordmark on dark  (what ships today)
  3. Mono black    single-colour, for stamps and invoices
  4. Mono white    single-colour, for dark partner lockups
  5. Mark only     already exists as logo-mark.svg


CLEARSPACE AND MINIMUM SIZE
---------------------------
  Clearspace:  the width of the top node (11 units on the 64 grid)
  Min width:   24px for the mark, 120px for the full lockup
  Never:       rotate, stretch, outline, add a shadow, or place the
               cyan strokes on a light background without the navy
