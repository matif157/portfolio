Beyond CV forwarding

A partner, not a channel.

Raisa Recruitment Group has run overseas employment promotions since 1997 and keeps one of the few dedicated head-hunting departments in the region. Thirty-one staff and ten sector consultants work out of the Kotwali Road office in Faisalabad, which also has dedicated interview and examination rooms.

That structure matters because a deployment is not a single hire. It is a medical cleared in one country, a visa stamped by another, a flight booked against a contract date, and a person who has to arrive ready. We own that whole chain.
